Welcome to Apni Dukaan Register, a mobile application operated by Kryzex Technologies ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal and business information of our users ("you" or "merchant").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information collected include:

  • Account & Contact Information: Mobile numbers, user names, or shop names voluntarily provided during registration for OTP authentication and profile setup.

We use the collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To improve app security and prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activities.

3. Data Storage and Security

We prioritize your data security. Your store entries are stored locally on your device and synchronized securely with encrypted cloud services. You have full control over your data. You can edit or delete customer records, transactions, and store items directly within the app. If you wish to delete your account or associated data permanently, you can write to us at contact@kryzex.com.
